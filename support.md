# Follow the Money — Support

Need help with the app, found a bug, or just want to share feedback? You're in the right place.

## Get in touch

The fastest way to reach me is by email:

**[mattagreen@gmail.com](mailto:mattagreen@gmail.com?subject=Follow%20the%20Money%20support)**

I'm a one-person operation, so please be patient — I aim to reply within a couple of days.

When you write in, including the following helps me help you faster:

- What iPhone you're using (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro)
- What version of iOS you're on (Settings → General → About → Software Version)
- The version of Follow the Money you're running (About tab in the app, near the bottom)
- A clear description of what you were doing and what went wrong
- A screenshot if anything looked weird

## Common questions

Most "is this normal?" questions are answered in the FAQ tab inside the app (tap the question-mark icon at the bottom of the screen). If yours isn't covered there, drop me an email.

## Privacy

Curious about what data the app collects (and doesn't)? See the [Privacy Policy](./).

## Feature requests

I'd genuinely love to hear what you wish the app did. Email me with the subject line **"Feature request"** and I'll add it to my list.

## Reporting a bug

For bugs, please include the steps to reproduce so I can recreate it on my end. If you can include a screen recording (Settings → Control Center → Screen Recording on iOS), even better.

## Refunds

Follow the Money is free, so there's nothing to refund. If you have a complaint about something Apple charged you for, that goes through the [Apple App Store refund process](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
